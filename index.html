Trending moments into Shorts, on autopilot

QuikShort is a desktop app for Windows. It finds what's trending, edits it into a vertical Short with captions and creator credit, and posts it to your YouTube channel at the times your audience is watching, after you approve it.

Finds trending clips

Watches the sources you choose, such as Reddit, Twitch clips and X, and ranks posts by how fast they're taking off.

Screens every candidate

Uses Claude, an AI model by Anthropic, to check brand safety, copyright risk and fit with your channel.

Edits for Shorts

Renders 1080×1920 vertical video with a hook, word-timed captions and a credit link to the original creator.

You stay in control

Every Short waits for your approval in the app or on Discord before it's posted. Nothing posts on its own unless you allow it.

Posts at the right time

Schedules uploads into your audience's peak hours and learns which hours work best for your channel.

Runs on your computer

Your keys, videos and statistics stay on your PC. QuikShort has no servers and no account system.

How QuikShort uses your YouTube account

When you click Connect YouTube in the app, you sign in with Google and grant QuikShort permission to:

  • Upload videos you have approved to your channel.
  • Read statistics (views and likes) for the videos QuikShort uploaded, so it can show you analytics and pick better posting times.
  • Update your channel description, if you leave the weekly bio refresh turned on.

QuikShort never deletes videos, reads your private messages or comments, or shares your Google data with anyone. Your sign-in token is stored only on your computer. You can disconnect at any time in your Google Account permissions. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
