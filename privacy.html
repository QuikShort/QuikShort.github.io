Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

QuikShort ("QuikShort", "we") is a desktop application for Windows that helps you turn trending content into YouTube Shorts and post them to your own YouTube channel. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and how you control it.

The short version: QuikShort runs entirely on your computer. We don't operate servers, we don't have user accounts, and we never receive your data. The app talks directly to the services you connect (such as Google/YouTube and Anthropic) using your own credentials.

1. Information the app uses

2. Google and YouTube data

When you click Connect YouTube, you sign in with Google and grant QuikShort these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload the videos you approve to your channel.
  • youtube: to read statistics for videos QuikShort uploaded and to update your channel description if you leave that feature on.

QuikShort uses this access only to provide those features to you. It does not delete videos, read your comments or messages, use your Google data for advertising, sell it, or transfer it to anyone else, and no person reads it. Your Google data is never sent to Anthropic or any other service.

QuikShort's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

QuikShort uses YouTube API Services. By using QuikShort's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can disconnect QuikShort from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

3. Services the app sends data to, at your direction

QuikShort connects directly from your computer to the services you set up. Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

  • Anthropic (Claude API): to screen candidates and write titles, descriptions and hashtags, the app sends the public post's title or text, its creator's public username, a transcript of the clip's audio, and for still images, the image. It does not send your Google data or personal information. Anthropic Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube: the videos you approve, with their title, description and tags.
  • Discord (optional): notifications to your own Discord direct messages, including video titles, descriptions, previews and, in manual posting mode, the finished videos.
  • Reddit, Twitch and X (optional): search requests for trending public content, made with your own API keys. QuikShort only reads from these services and never posts to them.

4. Storage and security

Everything QuikShort stores stays on your computer: settings and keys in the QuikShort folder, and the database, logs and videos in your Windows user profile (the QuikShort folder in your user profile). Keep your computer and these files secure, as anyone with access to them could use your connected accounts.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Edited videos are deleted automatically two days after upload, or when you reject them.
  • Statistics and logs stay on your computer until you delete them.
  • To delete everything, quit QuikShort, then delete the QuikShort folder and the QuikShort folder in your user profile, and revoke access in your Google account as described above.

6. Children

QuikShort is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for people who can lawfully operate a YouTube channel.

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
